Proteus simulation of the whole system

A connected and intelligent control and protection card for a generator set. It watches the mains, the fuel reserve, the engine temperature and the battery, protects the engine and sends every alert over MQTT.

ESP32MQTTMobile appAltium · Proteus

Industry 4.0 for power backup

A generator that fails silently is a risk for the whole plant. Overload, bad fuel, a flat battery, electrical faults and heat all threaten it. The answer is a card that measures, decides and reports in real time.

01

Mains watch

When the STEG supply is cut, the genset starts. When it returns, the genset stops.

02

Reserve

A float sensor keeps the tank full. At 20 % the pump starts and an alarm sounds.

03

Temperature

A Pt100 probe protects against overheating. Above 90 °C the motor and the pump stop.

04

Battery

Below 11.5 V the charger starts and the alarm is raised.

Software design

The software was built with Scrum in five sprints, one per safety function, and modelled with UML before coding.

Five sprints

  • 1. STEG: start the genset on a power cut, stop it when the mains return
  • 2. Reserve: keep the tank full with a pump
  • 3. Temperature: stop the motor above 90 °C
  • 4. Battery: start the charger below 11.5 V
  • 5. Alarm and send: buzzer, LED, LCD message and MQTT publication

Embedded hardware design

An ESP32 card with its sensors, indicators and relay, designed in Altium Designer.

Components and cost

  • ESP32 DevKit V1 and cable: 46.800 DT
  • LCD 4x20 with I2C: 32.000 DT
  • Buzzer: 6.000 DT
  • 2 potentiometers: 2.999 DT each
  • 5 buttons: 2.500 DT each
  • 4 LEDs: 0.100 DT each
  • Accessories: 20 DT
  • Total: 123.798 DT, about 130 DT for the whole project

Simulation and final product

Every state was simulated in Proteus, then built and tested in an aluminium box.
